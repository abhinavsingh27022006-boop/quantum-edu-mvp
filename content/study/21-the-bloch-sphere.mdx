# 21. The Bloch Sphere

The mathematical rules of quantum mechanics guarantee that a single qubit can always be perfectly mapped to a point on the surface of a 3D sphere.

Physicists call this the **Bloch Sphere**.
- The North Pole is `|0>`.
- The South Pole is `|1>`.
- The Equator is a perfect 50/50 superposition.

When we apply quantum gates like X, Y, and Z, we are literally grabbing this 3D sphere and rotating it! 
- An **X Gate** rotates the sphere 180 degrees around the X-axis (flipping North to South).
- A **Z Gate** rotates the sphere 180 degrees around the Z-axis (spinning it like a top, changing the phase).
- An **H Gate** rotates the sphere diagonally, shifting the poles to the equator.
