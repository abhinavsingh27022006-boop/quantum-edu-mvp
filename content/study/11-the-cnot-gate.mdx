# 11. The CNOT Gate

We've talked about qubits on their own, but how do they talk to each other?

In classical computing, we have the `IF / THEN` statement. If this is true, then do that.
In the quantum world, the most famous `IF / THEN` gate is the **CNOT** (Controlled-NOT).

The CNOT gate connects two qubits:
1. A **Control** qubit
2. A **Target** qubit

The rule is simple: **IF** the Control qubit is exactly `1`, **THEN** it flips the Target qubit (applies an X gate). If the Control is `0`, it does absolutely nothing.
