# 25. Quantum Cryptography (BB84)

If you send a secret text message over the internet, a hacker can copy the data packets and read them without you ever knowing.

But remember the rule of measurement? You cannot observe a quantum state without destroying its superposition.

If Alice sends a secret key to Bob using a stream of qubits, and a hacker (Eve) intercepts them and measures them, Eve destroys the delicate quantum states. 

When Bob receives the qubits, he and Alice will instantly notice that the error rate is far too high—the laws of physics literally alert them that someone is listening! This protocol is called **BB84**, and it guarantees perfectly secure communication backed by the laws of the universe.
