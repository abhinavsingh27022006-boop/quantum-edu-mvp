# 9. The Y-Axis Spin

The **Y Gate** (Yellow) is the weird cousin of the X and Z gates. It rotates the qubit around the Y-axis of the Bloch sphere, combining both a bit-flip (like X) and a phase-flip (like Z), while also multiplying by the imaginary number `i`.

Because of how probability works, if you just apply a Y gate to a 0 state and measure it, the complex phase disappears in the math, and it looks exactly like an X gate!

### Your Task
1. Drag a **Y Gate** to Q0.
2. Run the simulation.
3. Notice that the state is 100% `10`. Just like the X gate, it flipped the bit from 0 to 1, but under the hood, it also attached a complex phase!
