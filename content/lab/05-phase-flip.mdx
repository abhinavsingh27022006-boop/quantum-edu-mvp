# 5. The Invisible Mirror (Phase Flip)

The **Z Gate** (Pink) is a phase flip. In our physical world, it's like spinning a coin while it's in the air. 

If the coin is just lying on the table, spinning it doesn't change what side is facing up. The probabilities stay exactly the same when you look at it.

### Your Task
1. Drag a **Z Gate** onto **Q0**.
2. Run the simulation.
3. Notice that the probabilities don't change at all (it stays 100% `00`). The phase flip is invisible... until we combine it with superposition!
