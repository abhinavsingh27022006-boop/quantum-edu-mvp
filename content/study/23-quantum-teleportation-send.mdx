# 23. Teleportation Send

Alice has her entangled Qubit A, and her message Qubit M. Bob is on Mars with Qubit B.

**Step 3: The Interaction**
Alice entangles Qubit M with her Qubit A, and then she *measures* both of them. 
Measurement destroys the superposition of both Qubit M and Qubit A. The quantum state of the message seems to be gone forever!

But wait... because Qubit A was entangled with Qubit B on Mars, Alice's measurement instantly causes Bob's Qubit B to inherit the raw quantum state of Qubit M!

**Step 4: The Phone Call**
Bob's qubit is now holding the message, but it might be upside down or backwards depending on the random outcome of Alice's measurement. 

Alice must call Bob on a normal, classical telephone and say: *"I got a 0 and a 1."*
Using that two-bit code, Bob applies the correct X and Z gates to fix his qubit. The state is perfectly restored! The state teleported instantly, but Bob couldn't read it until the classical phone call arrived at the speed of light.
