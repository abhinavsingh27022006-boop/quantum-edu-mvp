# 14. The Z Gate (Phase Flip)

The **Pauli-Z Gate** (or **Z Gate**) is the strangest of the three Pauli gates.

If you apply a Z gate to a qubit that is resting at 0, absolutely nothing happens.
If you apply a Z gate to a qubit that is resting at 1, the probability chart still says it's 100% at 1.

So what does it do? It's an invisible mirror. 

The Z gate flips the **sign** (or phase) of the 1 state. It turns a `+1` into a `-1`. Since probabilities are calculated by squaring the amplitude (and a negative number squared is still positive!), the chart doesn't change. 

But this hidden negative sign is the secret weapon of quantum computing, as we'll see in the next session on Interference. Try it below: applying a Z does nothing to the chart!
