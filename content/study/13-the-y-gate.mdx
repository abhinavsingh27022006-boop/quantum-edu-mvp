# 13. The Y Gate

We know the X Gate flips our qubit from 0 to 1, exactly like a classical NOT gate.
But because qubits exist on a 3D sphere (the Bloch sphere), there's more than one way to reach the South Pole!

The **Pauli-Y Gate** (or **Y Gate**) also flips the qubit, but it does so by rotating it through a different axis.

Imagine turning a globe upside down. You could grab it by the sides and flip it over, or you could grab it by the front and back and tumble it over. 

Both methods result in the globe being upside down (a `1` state). The Y gate performs a flip, but simultaneously injects an invisible "phase" into the quantum wave. For simple probabilities, it looks exactly like an X gate!
