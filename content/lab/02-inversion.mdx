# The Coin Flip

Now that we know how to make a coin spin in the air, what if we just want to flip it over?

In classical computing, a NOT gate flips a `0` to a `1`, and a `1` to a `0`. It’s like flipping a coin that is resting on a table to show the opposite face.

In the quantum world, we use the **Pauli-X Gate** (or simply the **X Gate**) to achieve this. It acts exactly like a classical NOT gate when applied to our basic states, turning a `0` into a `1`.
