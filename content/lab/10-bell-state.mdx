# 10. The Anti-Correlated Bell State

The standard Bell state (`00` and `11`) means the qubits are perfectly correlated—if one is 0, the other is 0. 

But what if we want them to be perfectly anti-correlated? Meaning, if Q0 is 0, Q1 MUST be 1, and vice-versa? This means we want our probabilities to be exactly 50% `01` and 50% `10`.

### Your Task
1. Drag an **X Gate** to Q1 to flip it to 1 initially. (So our starting state becomes `01`).
2. Drag an **H Gate** to Q0 to put it into superposition.
3. Drag a **CX Gate** (Q0 controls Q1) to entangle them.
4. Run the simulation. You will see an anti-correlated state: exactly 50% `01` and 50% `10`. You did it! You've completed the Quantum Lab MVP!
