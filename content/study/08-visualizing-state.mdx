# 8. Visualizing State

How do physicists keep track of all this spinning and flipping? They use a visual tool called the **Bloch Sphere**.

Imagine the Earth. 
- The North Pole is the `0` state.
- The South Pole is the `1` state.

When a qubit is in a perfect 0, the arrow points straight up at the North Pole.
When we apply an **X Gate**, we are simply rotating that arrow 180 degrees all the way down to the South Pole (the 1 state).

When we apply an **H Gate**, we rotate the arrow exactly 90 degrees, so it points straight out at the Equator. Anywhere on the equator is a 50/50 superposition! 

Next time you hear about a "quantum state," just picture a tiny arrow pointing somewhere on a globe.
