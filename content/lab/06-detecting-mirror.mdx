# 6. Detecting the Mirror

In the previous puzzle, the phase flip (Z gate) was invisible because the coin wasn't in superposition. 

But if we split the coin (H), then flip the phase (Z), and then bring the split wave back together (H), the interference pattern changes drastically! The phases cancel out differently, and our "reversible randomness" ends up flipping the coin completely.

### Your Task
1. Drag an **H Gate** to Q0.
2. Drag a **Z Gate** right after it on Q0.
3. Drag another **H Gate** right after the Z on Q0.
4. Run the simulation. You will see that instead of returning to 0 (like in Puzzle 4), the phase flip caused the waves to interfere and produce a 1!
