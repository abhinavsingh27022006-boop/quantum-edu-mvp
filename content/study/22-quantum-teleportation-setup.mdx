# 22. Teleportation Setup

How do we actually perform Quantum Teleportation? It requires three qubits total.

**Step 1: The Entangled Link**
Alice and Bob must share a pair of entangled qubits. Think of these as a pair of magic walkie-talkies. Alice takes Qubit A, and Bob takes Qubit B. Bob gets on a spaceship and flies to Mars.

**Step 2: The Precious Message**
Alice has a third qubit, Qubit M (the Message). This qubit is in a beautiful, unknown superposition. Remember the No-Cloning theorem? Alice cannot copy Qubit M. 

If she wants to send Qubit M's state to Bob, she has to teleport it using the entangled link they created in Step 1.
