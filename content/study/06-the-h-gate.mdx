# 6. The Hadamard Gate

How do we actually put a qubit into superposition? We shoot it with a specific microwave pulse! 

In quantum programming, we represent this microwave pulse mathematically as a "Gate". The gate that creates a perfectly balanced spinning coin (50/50 superposition) is called the **Hadamard Gate** (or **H Gate**).

1. The qubit starts resting at 0.
2. Click to add an **H Gate**.
3. Look at the probability chart—it instantly splits 50/50!
