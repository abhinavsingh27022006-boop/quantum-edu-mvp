# Spooky Action at a Distance

Einstein famously called **Entanglement** "spooky action at a distance." Imagine having two magical coins. When you flip one coin and it lands on heads, the other coin instantly lands on heads too, no matter how far apart they are!

To entangle two qubits, we use a combination of the **H Gate** and the **CNOT Gate** (Controlled-NOT, or **CX Gate**).

The **CX Gate** links two qubits: a *control* and a *target*. If the control qubit is a `1`, it flips the target. If the control is `0`, it does nothing. But what happens if the control qubit is *spinning* in superposition?

### Your Task
Let's entangle Qubit 0 and Qubit 1!
1. Drag the **H Gate** onto **Qubit 0** to make it spin.
2. Drag the **CX Gate** onto **Qubit 1**. (It will automatically use Qubit 0 as the control).
3. Click **Run Simulation**. 

Look at the results! You will measure `00` (both tails) and `11` (both heads) with a 50/50 chance. But you will **never** see `01` or `10`. The qubits are completely linked!
