# 20. Teleportation Teaser

We just learned about the No-Cloning Theorem. You cannot copy a quantum state.

But what if Alice wants to send her precious quantum state to Bob, who is on the other side of the planet?
She can't copy it and email it. She can't read it (measure it), because that destroys the superposition.

So how do we move it? We use **Quantum Teleportation**.

By using a pair of entangled qubits (Spooky Action) shared between Alice and Bob, and a simple classical phone call, Alice can destroy her quantum state and instantly recreate it perfectly on Bob's qubit! 

It sounds like science fiction, but it is a standard quantum algorithm. In Module 3, we will build the exact circuit to make teleportation a reality.
