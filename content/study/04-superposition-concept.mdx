# 4. The Spinning Coin (Superposition)

Imagine a coin sitting on a table. If it's Heads up, let's call that `0`. If it's Tails up, let's call that `1`.

A classical bit is just a coin resting on the table. It is always exactly Heads, or exactly Tails.

But a **Qubit** can be spun!

When you spin a coin on the table, what is it? Is it Heads? Or is it Tails?
While it's spinning in a blur, it isn't really either. It's a combination of both at the same time.

In quantum mechanics, this "spinning coin" state is called **Superposition**. 
A qubit in superposition is doing math with both 0 and 1 simultaneously. This is where quantum computers get their massive parallel processing power.
