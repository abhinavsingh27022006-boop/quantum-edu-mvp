# The Magic Coin

Welcome to the quantum world. In classical computing, a bit is like a standard coin sitting on a table: it can be heads (1) or tails (0). 

A **Qubit** (quantum bit) can do something entirely different. It can spin in the air. 

While the coin is spinning, it isn't heads or tails—it is a combination of both. In quantum mechanics, we call this **Superposition**. We can put a qubit into superposition using the **Hadamard Gate** (or **H Gate**).
