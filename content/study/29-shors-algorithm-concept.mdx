# 29. Shor's Algorithm Concept

Every time you buy something online, your credit card is protected by RSA encryption. This security relies on a simple mathematical fact: it is easy to multiply two large prime numbers together, but it is practically impossible for a classical computer to figure out what those two prime numbers were if you only give it the final product.

In 1994, Peter Shor mathematically proved that a quantum computer could crack this encryption easily.

Instead of guessing prime numbers one by one, Shor's Algorithm turns the math problem into a problem of finding repeating patterns (periodicity). Quantum computers are incredibly good at finding patterns using interference!

By setting up a massive superposition and letting the repeating patterns constructively interfere, the quantum computer can find the hidden prime factors in minutes, a task that would take classical supercomputers thousands of years.
