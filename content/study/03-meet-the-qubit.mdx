# 3. Meet the Qubit

To build a quantum computer, we replace the classical "Bit" with a **Quantum Bit**, or **Qubit**.

A classical bit is like a light switch: it's either definitely OFF (0) or definitely ON (1). 

A qubit can also be 0 or 1. If you measure it, you will always get a 0 or a 1. But a qubit is made out of an actual quantum particle—like a single electron, or a photon of light.

Because it is a quantum particle, it doesn't have to choose between 0 and 1 until you look at it. It can exist in a fluid state *between* the two.

In the next session, we'll explore this fluid state, known as Superposition.
