# 1. What is a Computer?

To understand a quantum computer, we first need to look at the device you are using right now.

Deep down, inside the microchips of your phone or laptop, everything is just switches. Billions of tiny electrical switches called **transistors**.

## The Classical Bit
A switch can only be in one of two states:
- **Off** (0)
- **On** (1)

We call this a **Bit** (Binary Digit). Every photo you've ever taken, every movie you've ever watched, and every word you've ever typed is just a very long sequence of 0s and 1s.

A classical computer processes these bits one by one. It's incredibly fast, but it is fundamentally limited to dealing with definite, absolute states.

In the next session, we'll see why this "definite" nature becomes a huge problem when trying to simulate the universe.
